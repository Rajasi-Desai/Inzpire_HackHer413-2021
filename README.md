# Inzpire 

Video Demo - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t-qzDuUY4SI&ab_channel=RajasiDesai

## Info 
Our platform allows users to describe their problems, upload media (photos/ audio/ video), and react to each other's pain.
Users can browse different problems and take up a challenge of their liking. By taking up the challenge, hackers can contact the user who posted the challenge for more details. People can also view previous challenges and posts made by other users. Hence, they can learn innovative solutions from the global community of hackers.
Users are encouraged to post regularly updates on tackling the challenge.
Ideally, this will help to solve real-life problems and help hackers to gain experience find new and exciting opportunities.
